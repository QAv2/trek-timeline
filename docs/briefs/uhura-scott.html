Uhura and Scott: the thread, and what the SNW finale did with it

Research brief for the Chronometric Archive. Compiled 2026-09-25, the day after the finale was released. Data file: data/curated/threads/uhura-scott.json (22 Prime beats, 3 Kelvin beats).

Short answer

  • The finale is "Tomorrow's Enterprise," Strange New Worlds season 4, episode 10. Paramount+ released it on Thursday 24 September 2026.
  • In the present day (2262), Scotty asks Uhura out twice. Both times she sidesteps him, because her mind is on her career.
  • Her self from a year later, now serving under Kirk, has been seeing him in secret since the handover from Pike. She calls it casual but getting serious, and says neither of them expects it to last.
  • A change to the timeline erases Scotty. The two Uhuras work together to undo it, and the older one then kisses him in front of Kirk and Ortegas.
  • The episode leaves the present-day answer open. On screen she still hasn't said yes. The relationship is meant to start during the Pike-to-Kirk handover, and the six-episode final season (not expected before 2027) is set to show that handover.
  • The "no future in it" line is what connects the pairing to older canon. It lets the relationship be over by TOS, where the two are only colleagues, and it gives a history to the shore-leave dinner and the Sybok-driven flirtation in Star Trek V (2287). No later episode or film shows or mentions a relationship between them.

1. The finale
Episode "Tomorrow's Enterprise," SNW 4x10 (40th episode overall)
Released Thursday 24 September 2026, Paramount+ (the season ran weekly from 23 July)
Written by / directed by Henry Alonso Myers and Robbie Thompson / Chris Fisher
In-universe Present: stardate 2873.2, 2262 (Pike's Enterprise). Future: stardate 2876.2, c. 2263, "almost one year" later (Kirk's Enterprise)

How this was checked. I have not watched the episode. The beats below come from the published transcript (Scraps from the Loft). It is based on subtitles and only some lines have speaker labels. I checked it against the TrekMovie, SDCC Unofficial Blog, Gizmodo, TrekCore and Screen Rant recaps and against Memory Alpha's new episode and character pages.

Present day, 2262 (Pike's ship)

  1. The first invitation. Uhura beams back from a Starfleet protocols refresher course. Scotty is at the transporter and asks whether she'd like "a drink or something sometime." She answers: "Sorry, Scotty, I'm due back on the bridge. Talk later?" Later she admits to her future self that she "pretended not to hear him."
  2. A scream from the future. A pulse from a white hole lets Uhura hear Scotty shouting her name at a moment that hasn't happened yet. La'An warns her not to interfere with the future. Uhura won't let it go: "It's Scotty."
  3. The second invitation, misheard. Uhura asks Scotty for antimatter for a year-long experiment, a dilithium diamond. A year later, that diamond powers the drones that save him. He asks again: a drink, a meal, a snack? Her future self, listening over the link, says "Say yes." Uhura mutters "What? No," which is aimed at her future self, but Scotty takes it as a no to him. She gets as far as "Just be safe, okay?" When her future self asks why she refused, she says she doesn't like being pressured.
  4. The older Uhura hugs the younger Scotty. The two Uhuras swap places, so the older one ends up in 2262. Scotty tells her he thought she was avoiding him, and she hugs him. Pike notices ("I saw you hug him. Twice."). Scotty gives her the key insight that the signal is "like half a note."
  5. The ending. The space baby is born and its "Midwives" restore the timeline. They say only the two Uhuras will remember what changed. Present-day Uhura's closing log ends: "I may not know what the next few years will bring, but I know who I want to be with. My family." She joins Pike's dinner, and the future Uhura joins Kirk's poker game (per TrekMovie). The episode shows no answer to Scotty.

Future, c. 2263 (Kirk's ship)

  1. A secret couple. In a turbolift, Scotty kisses her cheek and says he'd never let anything happen to her. She says no kissing on the bridge or on missions. He asks, "do we have to stay hidden?" Per SDCC, they haven't gone public, which disappoints Scotty.
  2. How it started, in her words. On a shuttle into a new black hole, guest pilot Ortegas, now a flight instructor on Bindar XII, notices at once ("Okay, lovebirds"). Uhura tells her: "With the crew changing, Pike leaving, Jim taking command, we sort of held on to each other." She also says: "We've been casual this last year. Now it's getting serious." And: "I know it's not going to last. So does he." Ortegas: "Look, there's a 'now' in it."
  3. Scotty erased. Scotty had set the shuttle up so that any emergency beam-out must be one person at a time. The shuttle breaks up, and Uhura is beamed out first. She finds herself in a changed timeline. There, Scotty died a year earlier, in 2262, when his console exploded near the white hole. Later, Chapel tells Uhura she had been with him then and sang to him.
  4. The kiss. After the timeline is restored, Scotty and Ortegas beam in "right behind" her, and Uhura kisses him in the transporter bay. Memory Alpha and the SDCC and Gizmodo recaps put Kirk and Ortegas there, and Screen Rant says Kirk and Spock. In the transcript, Kirk speaks, and a wolf whistle follows.

What was left open

  • The present-day answer. Uhura in 2262 hasn't said yes on screen. The older Uhura's account places the start of the relationship "this last year," around Pike's departure.
  • How it ends before TOS. The episode gives only "I know it's not going to last. So does he."
  • Whether this future is fixed. Within the story it is the restored, "correct" timeline. The cast and producers are less definite, and three comments leave room for season 5 to arrive there differently:
  • Celia Rose Gooding says it was shot as a three-year gap and changed to one year.
  • Akiva Goldsman says they made "quick substitutions, switches, changes" so the episode wouldn't "entirely open up 'TOS' before it was time."
  • Jess Bush calls it "the potential future for the Enterprise."
  • Other details in the same future. Pike has been made an admiral; Uhura gives this away when she calls him "Admiral" in 2262. La'an's fate is withheld. M'Benga is spending time on Vulcan and McCoy ("Bones") is about to take over. Ortegas is a captain. Chapel still plans to marry Roger Korby.

What it appears to set up

Season 5 is the final season: six episodes, ordered June 2025 and filmed September to December 2025. When it was ordered, it was expected to premiere at least two years later. Goldsman has said it will show how the characters become who they are at the start of TOS, including Kirk's first day in command as Pike's successor (reported by Wikipedia). That handover is where the older Uhura places the start of the relationship. No one on the production has said the romance will be shown. In TVLine, Myers was asked directly whether they would explore it in the final season and answered only about why they created it. The nearest statements:

  • Gooding (Screen Rant) was speaking about the gap between the two Uhuras in general: "We'll see it in season five. We'll see pieces of it, but we're not going to see everything…"
  • Goldsman (TVLine), on season 5: "we will locate everybody, both canon and non-canon characters."

2. The lead-up inside SNW: very little

  • Season 2 finale, "Hegemony" (2261). Scotty is introduced. He escaped the Gorn attack on the Stardiver and comes aboard. He and Uhura share no scene.
  • Season 3 (2261).
  • "A Space Adventure Hour" gives them their first real scene as a pair. Scotty brings Uhura a "hypothetical" about La'An being trapped on the holodeck, and she works out how he can reach her.
  • In "The Sehlat Who Ate Its Tail," both are part of Kirk's makeshift crew on the Farragut.
  • Uhura's romance in season 3 is with Beto Ortegas, Erica's brother. It runs through "Wedding Bell Blues," "Through the Lens of Time," "What Is Starfleet?" and "Four-and-a-Half Vulcans." That last episode includes the Vulcan-Uhura mind-meld, an apology, and his last five hours aboard. He doesn't appear in season 4.
  • In the finale, it is Erica Ortegas who tells Uhura there's a "now" in her relationship with Scotty.
  • Season 4, episodes 1–9 (2262). I read all nine transcripts. The pair share two small moments and nothing romantic:
  • In "Human Best Friend," Scotty worries about a week without tricorders and Uhura says she'll keep working. Later, intoxicated by the planet's air, she makes Scotty and Ortegas apologize to each other.
  • In "Off-Hour," with comms down, she notes that even Scotty isn't answering, "and he always picks up."

Within SNW, then, the romance arrives in the finale with almost no build-up. The groundwork it relies on is Star Trek V. TV Guide describes 2262 Uhura as "resisting entering a fledgling shipboard romance," but the earlier episodes don't show one. Reviews differed. Den of Geek called it "honestly adorable." TrekCore's Alex Perry defended it against canon objections by pointing to Star Trek V.

Star Trek V in detail (checked against the transcript):

  • Uhura: "And I thought we were supposed to be going together." She means shore leave. She has brought dinner, and Scotty tells her: "Oh lass, you're the most understanding woman I know."
  • Later, under Sybok's influence, she sits with him after he has knocked himself out on a bulkhead. She says Sybok has put them in touch with feelings they were afraid to express, and "there's so much I want to tell you." He puts her off until he's stronger: "I don't think I could take it in my present condition. ...Or yours."

Left out as too thin to plot:

  • Routine bridge exchanges throughout TOS.
  • TAS "Bem" (2270), where Uhura, in command, orders Scotty and Sulu back aboard.
  • Star Trek III, where they play separate parts in taking the Enterprise.
  • The Motion Picture and Star Trek II, which give them no scene of their own.

Later canon:

  • Generations (2293): Scotty attends the Enterprise-B launch without Uhura.
  • TNG "Relics" (2369): Scotty never mentions Uhura. I checked the full transcript and she isn't named once.
  • Uhura's later career, from Picard set dressing: captain of the USS Leondegrance from 2301 to her retirement in 2333.
  • The two appear together once more, as holograms among Dal's simulated crew in Prodigy "Kobayashi" (2383). These are simulations, not the characters.

4. How the SNW beats fit later canon

Where they fit

  • Star Trek V is the anchor, and it fits well. Uhura planned shore leave "together," she brings dinner, and he calls her "the most understanding woman I know." Those lines read naturally as two people with a past. The finale gives them one, and on its framing (casual, never expected to last) there's no need for them to have been a couple between 2263 and 2287. That matches Myers's stated aim (section 5).
  • TOS showing nothing is consistent with "not going to last." During the five-year mission Scotty is interested in other women: Carolyn Palamas ("Who Mourns for Adonais?", 2267), Kara ("Wolf in the Fold", 2267) and Mira Romaine ("The Lights of Zetar", 2269). Early TOS Uhura flirts with Spock, not Scotty ("The Man Trap" and "Charlie X", 2266). If the SNW relationship ended between c. 2263 and 2266, nothing on screen contradicts it.
  • Star Trek VI and "Relics" point to separate lives. In 2293 he has bought a boat and she is going to the Academy. In 2369 Scotty reminisces about Kirk and the old ship and doesn't name her.

Where they strain

  • The dates around Kirk. The finale's future has Kirk in command c. 2263, with Uhura aboard and McCoy about to join. TOS's "Where No Man Has Gone Before" (2265) has Scotty aboard but not Uhura (Alden is on communications) and Dr. Piper as chief medical officer. MA usually starts Kirk's five-year mission in 2264 or 2265. This is the main reason the cast and producers hedge on the future (section 1).
  • Nichelle Nichols's own reading. In a 1990 interview (Star Trek: The Official Fan Club Magazine #73, April/May 1990, as summarized by Memory Alpha), she said Uhura and Scotty were just friends in Star Trek V, with no romantic interest. That is behind-the-scenes material, not canon, but SNW now reads the scenes the other way.
  • The sickbay scene can be read two ways. In the film, Uhura's confession is driven by Sybok, and Scotty treats it as not really her. SNW's reading is that it was leftover feeling brought to the surface. Both readings fit the film.
  • SNW's own build-up is thin (section 2). The finale depends on Star Trek V more than on anything SNW itself set up.

5. What the producers and cast have said (behind the scenes, not canon)

  • Henry Alonso Myers, co-showrunner (TVLine, 24 Sep 2026): "So there has always been a flirtation. It kind of came out more in the movies…" Also: "What we were trying to do was just reach out and imagine a deeper version of the thing that didn't get to make it on screen." And: "we thought it would be nice to imply that the things that we had seen already had a purpose in the past."
  • Myers (TV Guide, 24 Sep 2026): "we wanted to imply that this has been a journey that has been happening for some time."
  • Akiva Goldsman, co-showrunner (TV Guide): he lists "Scotty and Uhura — in the movies" among the relationships TOS only seeded. He adds that "Henry took a deep cut with the mention of the Scotty-Uhura thing."
  • Goldsman (TVLine): for a while "Tomorrow's Enterprise" was going to be the last episode of the series, until the final season was negotiated. That is why it reaches ahead to Kirk's ship.
  • Celia Rose Gooding (TV Guide, 24 Sep 2026):
  • She wanted to remove the idea that "the Star Trek V relationship came out of nowhere," and to give the "Uhotty" shippers "a little bit more wind in the sail."
  • She describes the older Uhura as "craving to be with him again," while the SNW-era Uhura "thinks that she's not ready."
  • Gooding (Screen Rant, 24 Sep 2026): "when we originally shot it, it was a three-year difference between the two of them, not just one year."
  • Jess Bush (Screen Rant, 24 Sep 2026): "stepping into the potential future for the Enterprise."
  • A Variety interview with Gooding exists but is paywalled. A Washington Blade repost of it has no discussion of the romance.

6. Kelvin timeline (separate; not part of the Prime thread)

In the Kelvin films Uhura's romance is with Spock. Scotty is never paired with her: his constant companion is Keenser, and in Beyond he works with Jaylah. TrekCore's review makes the same contrast: the Kelvin films paired Spock and Uhura, while the TOS films hinted at Uhura and Scotty.

Year Source (MA title) Beat W
2258 Star Trek (film) She gets herself posted to the Enterprise; after Vulcan is destroyed she comforts Spock in a turbolift with a kiss, and their relationship is revealed 3
2259 Star Trek Into Darkness Over Qo'noS she confronts Spock, in front of Kirk, about his apparent indifference to dying at Nibiru 2
2263 Star Trek Beyond They've broken up (McCoy: "You guys break up?"); the vokaya amulet he gave her helps locate the crew; they begin to renew it 2

Beyond is set in 2263, the same year as the finale's future. So in that year the Prime Uhura is secretly with Scotty while the Kelvin Uhura is between breakup and reconciliation with Spock. Prime canon has its own trace of the Spock pairing in early TOS flirting ("The Man Trap," "Charlie X"). That is the thread the Kelvin films built on.

7. Echoes I noticed (my observations; the production hasn't claimed them)

  • "The Changeling" (2267). Uhura's singing draws Nomad to her. Scotty tries to pull it off her, is killed, and is then brought back. In the finale's erased timeline Scotty dies with Uhura singing to him, the aliens speak in music, and his death is undone.
  • Star Trek IV (2286). Scotty can beam people out only "one at a time," Uhura goes first, and someone is left behind. The finale uses the same setup: one-at-a-time beam-outs from the shuttle, Uhura first, Scotty "right behind you."

8. Notes and limits

  • Premiere date. Paramount+ and Wikipedia say season 4 premiered 23 July 2026 and MA says 24 July, probably a time-zone or regional difference. All sources agree the finale was released 24 September 2026, a Thursday.
  • Could not verify:
  • Who exactly witnesses the kiss. MA, SDCC and Gizmodo point to Kirk and Ortegas, and Screen Rant says Kirk and Spock. The JSON says Kirk and Ortegas.
  • Whether present-day Scotty is at Pike's closing dinner.
  • Any on-screen action the transcript doesn't describe.
  • Any plans for the pair in season 5.
  • Memory Alpha's coverage of season 4 is incomplete. Most season 4 episode pages have no summaries yet, so I checked the season 4 lead-up against transcripts. MA's finale page and its Scott page were updated after airing and agree with the transcript on the main points. One minor wording issue: MA's Scott page describes their 2262 relationship as already casual, but on screen that belongs to the months after the present-day scenes.
